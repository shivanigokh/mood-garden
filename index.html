MOOD GARDEN

Your face changes the weather.

The garden asks to use your camera. Everything is processed on this device. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded.

camera off

Face

  • Smile flowers open, warm light. Bigger smile, fuller bloom.
  • Blink a drift of petals falls.
  • Raise eyebrows the wind picks up.
  • Open mouth a pulse ripples outward.
  • Tilt head the world tilts with you.
  • Furrow brows (angry) a storm rolls in.
  • Frown (sad) rain falls, plants droop.
  • Relax the garden settles back to rest.

Hands

  • Open palm draws light and petals to you.
  • Fist pushes them away.
  • Pinch plants a new flower.
  • Wave sends a gust of wind.
