MIRROR PET

Pip copies every face you make.

Smile, wink, pout, puff your cheeks or pull a grumpy face, and Pip does it back and reacts. The camera is processed on this device and nothing is recorded or uploaded.

starting camera…

Pip copies you

  • Tilt and turn your head and Pip does too.
  • Blink or wink and Pip winks back.
  • Open your mouth and so does Pip.

And reacts

  • Smile blushes and bounces. Laugh for giggles.
  • Raise your brows with an open mouth: surprise!
  • Frown and Pip gets sad and teary.
  • Scowl and Pip turns red and steams.
  • Pout a kiss for hearts.
  • Puff your cheeks and Pip puffs up.
  • Look away for a while and Pip dozes off.
  • Click Pip for a boop.
