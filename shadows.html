SHADOWS

Make a shape. Watch it come alive.

Hold your hand up in front of the camera and its shadow falls on the wall. Make a dog, rabbit, deer, swan, elephant or more and hold it still: the animal jumps off your hand and plays with you. Hand tracking runs on this device and nothing is recorded or uploaded.

starting camera…

Make a shape and hold it

  • Dog index and little finger up, middle fingers tucked.
  • Rabbit two fingers up in a V.
  • Deer three fingers up, little finger tucked.
  • Cat thumb and little finger out.
  • Elephant a thumbs-up.
  • Snake point one finger.
  • Swan an OK sign: thumb and index tips touching.
  • Bird two open hands, thumbs hooked side by side.
  • Butterfly two open hands crossed at the wrists.
  • Crocodile two flat hands, one above the other, like jaws.

Then play

  • Hold your hand low to be sniffed, nuzzled or snapped at.
  • Stroke over them with an open hand to pet them.
  • Hold it higher and they jump, rear up or trumpet. Birds and butterflies land on a still hand.
  • Swipe fast to startle them.
